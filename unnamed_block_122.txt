**Key fields Antigravity will actually use on wake‑up:**

- **`goal`** — to restate what it’s doing.  
- **`plan_steps` + `current_step_index`** — to know where it is in the plan.  
- **`context.files_generated` / `files_pending`** — to avoid duplicating work.  
- **`context.next_actions`** — to resume with a clear next step.  
- **`context.diagnostics_summary` + `crash_events`** — to explain the crash and mitigation.  

You can store this as a document in RT/timeslip or your mesh ledger keyed by `session_id`.
